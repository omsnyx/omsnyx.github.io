The Effect of Crab Mentality on Learning and Strategies for Avoiding It Among Grade 11 Students of Mapúa-Makati

Group research paper, FIL02 (Reading and Analysis of Various Texts Toward Research) · May 2025 · written in Filipino

A group research study by eight Senior High School students at Mapúa University, Makati. We used a mixed-method design, combining a survey with semi-structured interviews, to examine how crab mentality (pulling others down out of envy) affects Grade 11 students' learning, relationships, and confidence, and to identify strategies for preventing it. The study was grounded in Malicious Envy Theory.

Most respondents linked crab mentality to envy of others' success, and most said teaching growth mindset and valuing one's own progress is the most effective way to prevent it.
